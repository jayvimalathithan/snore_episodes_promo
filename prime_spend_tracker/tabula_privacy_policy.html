Privacy Policy for Tabula Expense App

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Developer: CALX Labs / Prime Vector Labs

Package Name: com.calxlabs.tabula

1. Introduction

CALX Labs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Tabula Expense App (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application. We built Tabula with an offline-first architecture—your personal financial records belong strictly to you and remain on your device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • Financial & Transaction Data: All expenses, income records, transfers, categories, recurring schedules, and payment methods you enter (manually, via voice/NLP parsing, or via receipt OCR scanning) are stored exclusively locally on your device using an internal SQLite database. We do not transmit, upload, sync, or store your financial transactions on any external servers or cloud databases.
  • Backups & Exports: When you choose to export your database (CSV backups) or financial reports (PDF, Excel, CSV), the files are generated locally and saved directly to your device’s local storage (Downloads/TabulaExpense or Downloads/TabulaReport). You retain full control over these files.

3. Device Permissions We Use

To provide core offline features, the App may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Biometric / Device Credentials (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used solely to authenticate you locally via fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN when opening the App or returning after the background grace period. Biometric data is processed entirely by your device's operating system; the App never accesses or stores raw biometric signatures.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you actively trigger voice input to parse spoken transactions. Speech-to-text processing occurs via your device's local/OS speech recognition engine, and audio is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Camera & Storage / Photos: Used when you scan receipts for local Optical Character Recognition (OCR) text extraction or when saving and importing backup CSVs and financial reports. OCR processing runs locally on your device via Google ML Kit.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used locally on your device to alert you about upcoming credit card bill due dates and pending recurring transactions.

4. Third-Party Services (Ads & In-App Purchases)

While your financial data stays offline, the App integrates standard third-party SDKs to support free access and premium upgrades:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display rewarded video advertisements for free-tier access sessions. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and general diagnostic data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: Used to process and verify Lifetime Premium in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card or banking details.

5. Data Security and Retention

Because all financial records reside locally on your device:

  • You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backup files exported to your Downloads folder.
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete the local SQLite database unless you have previously exported a CSV backup.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
