# Privacy Policy for Snore Episodes : Count & Duration

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

## 1. Overview
"Snore Episodes : Count & Duration" ("we", "our", or "the App") is built as a personal sleep-tracking application. We value your privacy: all sound processing, snore counting, duration logging, and habit impact calculations take place locally on your personal device.

## 2. Medical & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer
- **Not a Medical Device:** "Snore Episodes : Count & Duration" is an informational self-tracking tool and is **NOT** a medical device, diagnostic software, or medical solution.
- **No Medical Advice or Diagnosis:** The App does not diagnose, treat, mitigate, prevent, or cure any sleep disorders, medical conditions, or health issues (such as Sleep Apnea). 
- **Informational & Habit Tracking Purposes Only:** All metrics—including snore counts, severity categorizations (mild, medium, severe), durations, and habit impact factors—are estimates calculated locally based strictly on user-selected habits and acoustic signal thresholds on your device.
- **Consult a Healthcare Professional:** This App should never replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider if you have concerns about your sleep health, snoring patterns, or suspect you have a sleep disorder. Never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice based on metrics recorded by this App.

## 3. Microphone & Audio Processing
- **Local Audio Analysis:** The App requires access to your device's microphone solely to detect sound levels, count snoring episodes, and record episode durations while you sleep.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** Audio is processed entirely on your device in real time. We do not stream, upload, transmit, or back up audio recordings to external servers or third parties.

## 4. In-App Purchases & Third-Party Services
While core snore analysis runs offline, the App utilizes third-party services to process in-app purchases and manage subscription features:
- **RevenueCat & Google Play Billing:** We use RevenueCat to process payments, handle subscription status, and validate digital purchase receipts through the Google Play Store.
- **Data Processed for Purchases:** RevenueCat collects anonymous purchase history and generated app user identifiers purely for subscription verification and customer entitlement management. No audio data, habit logs, or sleep records are ever shared with RevenueCat.

## 5. Local Data Storage & Personal Habits
- **Local Data Storage:** All recorded snore logs, duration metrics, timestamps, and user-entered habits remain stored strictly on your local device storage.
- **No Account Required:** The App does not require account creation, social log-ins, or cloud backends.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect or transmit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 7. Data Erasure
You can erase all app metrics, habit logs, and recorded episode history at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android system settings or uninstalling the application.
